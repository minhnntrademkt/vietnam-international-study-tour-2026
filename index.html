Executive Overview

Why Vietnam? A Story of Bold Reform & Digital Momentum

Join the Australian Doctors Business Academy for an extraordinary week exploring the real-world transformation of Vietnam's healthcare system from the ground up.

Vietnam's healthcare trajectory over the last decade represents one of Southeast Asia's most ambitious models of universal care rollout, grassroots primary care, and public-private innovation.

Over the last decade, Vietnam has achieved over 90% population coverage under its National Health Insurance scheme while simultaneously pioneering nationwide digital medical records, AI diagnostics, and telemedicine networks connecting central tertiary hospitals to rural commune health stations.
